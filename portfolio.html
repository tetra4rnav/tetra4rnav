About

Physician-researcher working on structural determinants of health, causal inference, and health services utilization.

Trained in internal medicine at Niigata University Hospital, I observed repeatedly that patients could not sustain care not due to cost alone, but structural and social barriers upstream. That observation pulled me toward social epidemiology and public health systems research.

At Hopkins I am developing methods-heavy work at the intersection of health services utilization, structural racism measurement, and interrupted time series design. My fieldwork has taken me to Ghana, the Philippines, and rural Japan.

Cannabis dispensary proximity and binge drinking — Baltimore City
Ecological study across 194 census tracts using CDC PLACES 2025. Sequential OLS with Index of Concentration at the Extremes (ICE) structural adjustment. Approximately 95% attenuation of the crude association after full structural adjustment. Submitted to SAREP 2026.
